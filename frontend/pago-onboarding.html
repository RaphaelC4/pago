01 — Why Pago

Splits you can check. Receipts you can trust.

The fee
0.5%

Flat, on the payout

No subscription, no listing fee. The fee moves with the payment itself — 99.5% of every claim lands with you.

Receipts

Read from the events, not recomputed

Every amount on a receipt comes out of the claim transaction's own payout events — fee, each seat, the claim hash. What the chain said is what you see.

To pay

Their wallet is the account.

No sign-up, no Pago app. Open the link, approve in your wallet. You need USDT0 or USDG on X Layer.

Network

Built on X Layer/TapeOut

Stablecoin payments settle on X Layer, with the contract addresses published for anyone to read back before they trust a thing.
